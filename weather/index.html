Weather

For as long as I can remember, I have had a fascination with the weather. I love watching storms from the comfort of my home. The lightning flashing through the windows, the thunder rumbling and shaking the entire house. Just something about it is exciting.

I have set up my own weather station on my property to capture local weather data. This is mostly for fun, but I also plan on using it to automate some things via Home Assistant once I have enough data. I also plan on supplying this data to the NWS for their observation as well.
